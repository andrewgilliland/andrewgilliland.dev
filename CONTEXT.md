# andrewgilliland.dev

Andrew Gilliland's portfolio and writing site: articles, case studies, and a resume.

## Language

### Writing

**Article**:
A piece of writing in the articles collection, authored as Markdown or MDX.
_Avoid_: Post, blog post, note

**Draft**:
An article marked `draft: true` in its frontmatter. Drafts are never listed, linked, syndicated, or given a share image, in any environment.
_Avoid_: Unpublished article, WIP

**Published article**:
An article that is not a draft. Everything readers see (listings, article pages, RSS, share images) shows published articles only.
_Avoid_: Live article, public article

**Article shapes**:
The four overlapping, brightly coloured shapes that belong to an article. They're derived from the article itself, so the same article shows the same shapes wherever it appears (its card, the home page's latest-article tile).
_Avoid_: Decoration, card decoration, card shapes

**Topic**:
A subject an article is filed under. Topics are written as `tags` in frontmatter, but the site and its code call them topics.
_Avoid_: Tag, category, label
